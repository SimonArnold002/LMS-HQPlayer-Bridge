PLUGIN_HQPLAYER_BRIDGE
	EN	HQPlayer Bridge

PLUGIN_HQPLAYER_BRIDGE_DESC
	EN	Presents each HQPlayer instance on your network as an ordinary Lyrion player, driven directly over HQPlayer's own control API. No UPnP bridge and no helper in the playback path: HQPlayer fetches the stream straight from Lyrion, so the plugin adds no audio stage of its own. Most of your library is handed over untouched, but Lyrion does still convert formats HQPlayer cannot decode, such as ALAC and AAC. Instances are discovered automatically - or, with discovery switched off in the settings, taken from IP addresses you enter.

PLUGIN_HQPLAYER_ADDR_TITLE
	EN	HQPlayer addresses

PLUGIN_HQPLAYER_ADDR_DESC
	EN	The IP address of each HQPlayer to use, separated by commas, spaces or new lines. IP addresses only, not host names. HQPlayer must be running at a new address when you save - the Bridge connects to it and will not save an address nothing answers at. A player is identified by the name set in HQPlayer, not by its address: delete an address and save to remove its player, and add it back - or replace it with HQPlayer's new address - to get the same player back with its settings and playlist.

PLUGIN_HQPLAYER_AUTO_TITLE
	EN	Automatically

PLUGIN_HQPLAYER_AUTO_DESC
	EN	Automatically: the Bridge looks for HQPlayer on this network and follows an HQPlayer whose address changes. Only the addresses below: for an HQPlayer on another subnet, or on a network that blocks multicast - the Bridge then sends no discovery traffic at all, so HQPlayer's log gets no discovery lines, and any player not in the list is removed when you save. The two never run together: going back to automatic clears the list and removes those players, and discovery finds again whichever it can reach, with their settings. With only the addresses, an HQPlayer whose address changes is not followed: update the address, or give that machine a fixed address.
