PLUGIN_HQPLAYER_BRIDGE
	EN	HQPlayer Bridge

PLUGIN_HQPLAYER_BRIDGE_DESC
	EN	Presents each HQPlayer instance on your network as an ordinary Lyrion player, driven directly over HQPlayer's own control API. No UPnP bridge and no external helper binary: no audio passes through this plugin at all, because HQPlayer fetches the stream straight from Lyrion. There is nothing to configure - instances are discovered automatically.

PLUGIN_HQPLAYER_TIER1
	EN	Playing the original file straight from Lyrion, untranscoded - tags and cover art come from the file itself.

PLUGIN_HQPLAYER_TIER3
	EN	Playing a local file that HQPlayer cannot decode itself, transcoded by Lyrion on the way past. Pre-queued, so track joins stay seamless.

PLUGIN_HQPLAYER_TIER4
	EN	Playing through the Lyrion player stream, used for radio and for services that cannot hand out a direct link. One stream per player, so track joins are not pre-queued.

PLUGIN_HQPLAYER_TIER5
	EN	Playing a streaming service directly - HQPlayer fetches the service's own URL, with no Lyrion proxy or transcode in between. Pre-queued, so track joins stay seamless.
